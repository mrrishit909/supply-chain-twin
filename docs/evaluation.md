# Evaluation

Generated by `python -m sc.evaluate` on the full synthetic network: 500 suppliers, 50 facilities, 5,000 parts,
294,897 shipments over two years (38,606 open or planned). All data is synthetic: the numbers show that each component beats its
baseline on this generated world, not how it would do on a real supply chain.

## 1. Arrival-time forecast

Trained on 120,000 deliveries, tested on the most recent 180 days (54,214 deliveries) it never saw.

| | Mean absolute error |
|---|---|
| Gradient boosting, median (`eta-gb-1.0.0`) | 1.42 days |
| Baseline: lane median | 1.94 days |
| Baseline: the promised date | 1.93 days |

The 80% interval (10th to 90th percentile models) contained the actual arrival 79% of the time and is 4.27 days wide on average.

## 2. Supplier delay probability

Late = more than 3 days after the promised date (12.4% of test deliveries).

| | AUC | Brier score |
|---|---|---|
| Calibrated gradient boosting (`supplier-delay-gb-1.0.0`) | 0.827 | 0.0861 |
| Baseline: overall late rate | 0.5 | 0.1137 |

## 3. Recovery plans

Shanghai capacity cut at four depths and lengths. Net cost = lost margin + plan cost, in the expected case, judged by the daily simulator.

| Capacity lost | Days | Shipments delayed | Max delay | Do nothing | Fly the most delayed first | Optimized (MIP) | Solve time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 50% | 14 | 2,322 | 5.2 d | $0.00M | $2.40M | $0.00M | 2.2 s (optimal) |
| 70% | 21 | 5,122 | 13.1 d | $1.41M | $5.38M | $0.22M | 3.3 s (optimal) |
| 90% | 21 | 6,050 | 18.3 d | $11.08M | $7.39M | $0.76M | 5.3 s (optimal) |
| 70% | 42 | 6,050 | 26.2 d | $41.21M | $7.75M | $1.41M | 16.3 s (optimal) |

The model for the 70% / 21-day case has 78,437 variables and 34,775 constraints (HiGHS).
The optimizer plans for the expected delay only. Under uncertainty its cheap plan keeps less slack than flying everything; the demo's last step shows that tail.

## 4. Monte Carlo

1,000 futures for the 70% / 21-day cut take 4.0 s. Lost revenue: 10th percentile $0.07M, median $3.34M, 90th percentile $36.49M.
With the same transit noise and no port cut, lost revenue is zero in every future: the network was generated with enough safety stock for ordinary variation.
